# Reflex — Product Roadmap

## Purpose

The Reflex MVP focuses on one core problem: helping small electronics retailers coordinate deliveries through a shared workflow instead of relying on scattered WhatsApp messages and phone calls.

Future development should build on this foundation gradually, based on user feedback and validated needs.

---

## Phase 1 — MVP

**Goal: Establish a reliable delivery coordination workflow.**

The MVP provides:

* Delivery request creation
* Customer and delivery details
* Dispatcher view of open requests
* Manual rider assignment
* Rider delivery view
* Delivery status updates
* Delivery confirmation
* Delivery history

**Success indicator:**

Retailers, dispatchers and riders can complete a delivery from request to confirmation using one shared system.

---

## Phase 2 — Communication & Reliability

**Goal: Reduce the need for users to manually check the system.**

Potential improvements:

* Push notifications for important status changes
* SMS notifications where appropriate
* Failed-delivery and cancellation states
* Improved delivery history
* Basic activity/audit history
* Better validation and error handling

**Why this comes next:**

Once the basic workflow is working, the next priority is making communication more reliable and reducing missed updates.

---

## Phase 3 — Smarter Delivery Operations

**Goal: Help dispatchers make better assignment decisions.**

Potential improvements:

* Rider availability
* Rider workload visibility
* Delivery priority
* Suggested rider assignment
* Basic route information
* Multiple-delivery coordination

**Why this comes next:**

Manual assignment is suitable for a small MVP, but it can become inefficient as delivery volume and the number of riders increase.

---

## Phase 4 — Location & Delivery Intelligence

**Goal: Improve visibility and operational decision-making.**

Potential improvements:

* Optional live GPS tracking
* Mapping integration
* Route optimization
* Delivery performance metrics
* Basic operational dashboards
* Estimated delivery times

**Why this comes later:**

These capabilities introduce greater technical, privacy and infrastructure requirements. They should be added after the core coordination workflow has been validated.

---

## Phase 5 — Scale & Integrations

**Goal: Support larger retailers and broader delivery operations.**

Potential improvements:

* Integration with retailer inventory/order systems
* Customer delivery notifications
* Payment integrations
* Offline support
* Multiple branches
* Role-based access controls
* Advanced analytics

**Why this comes later:**

These features support scale and integration with broader business processes, but they are not necessary to validate the initial Reflex value proposition.

---

## Roadmap Principle

Reflex follows a **KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid)** approach.

The product should not attempt to become a full logistics platform before proving that its core workflow solves the retailer's coordination problem.

The progression is:

**Coordinate → Communicate → Optimize → Scale**

Each phase builds on the previous one rather than adding complexity all at once.
