# Reflex — Product Definition

## 1. Product Overview

Reflex is a lightweight delivery coordination system designed for small electronics and accessories retailers in Nairobi.

It replaces scattered WhatsApp messages and phone calls with one shared workflow for creating, assigning, tracking and confirming deliveries.

The core workflow is:

**Request → Assign → Pick Up → Deliver → Confirm**

## 2. Target Customer

The MVP focuses on small electronics and accessories retailers in Nairobi who use riders to deliver customer orders locally.

Typical products include:

- Phone chargers
- Cables
- Earphones and headphones
- Power banks
- Phone cases
- Other small electronics accessories

## 3. Problem

Small retailers commonly coordinate deliveries through WhatsApp and phone calls.

As delivery volume increases, important information can become scattered across conversations. This makes it difficult to quickly know:

- Which deliveries are waiting to be assigned
- Which rider has been assigned to an order
- Whether an order has been picked up
- The current delivery status
- Whether the delivery has been completed

The result is limited visibility, repeated follow-up calls and difficulty maintaining a reliable delivery history.

## 4. Users

### Retailer Staff

Creates a delivery request containing:

- Customer name
- Customer phone number
- Delivery address
- Item description

The retailer can then view the delivery's current status.

### Dispatcher

The dispatcher:

- Views open delivery requests
- Assigns deliveries to riders
- Monitors delivery progress

### Rider

The rider:

- Views assigned deliveries
- Updates the delivery status
- Confirms completed deliveries

## 5. Core Delivery Workflow

A delivery moves through a simple sequence:

**Requested → Assigned → Picked Up → Delivered → Confirmed**

Each delivery has a record and a current status so that the team can see where it is in the process.

## 6. MVP Features

### Retailer

- Create delivery request
- View delivery status

### Dispatcher

- View open delivery requests
- Assign a rider
- Monitor delivery status

### Rider

- View assigned deliveries
- Update delivery status
- Confirm completed delivery

### Delivery Management

- Unique delivery record
- Delivery status tracking
- Delivery history

## 7. MVP Goal

The goal of the MVP is not to build a complete logistics platform.

The goal is to solve one core problem:

> **Give small retailers a shared and visible way to coordinate deliveries without relying on scattered WhatsApp messages and phone calls.**

## 8. Out of Scope

The following are intentionally excluded from the MVP:

- Live GPS tracking
- Automatic rider assignment
- Route optimization
- Offline mode
- Integrated payments
- Advanced analytics
- Customer accounts
- Complex automated notifications

These may be considered in later versions after the core workflow has been validated.
